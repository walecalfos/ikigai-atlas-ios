## Description

Ikigai (生き甲斐) is the Japanese idea of what makes life worth living. In Japan it isn't a single grand purpose. It's the people, pursuits and small moments that give life its worth, and the felt sense that it does.

Ikigai Atlas helps you find yours from the evidence of your own life, in six stages:

• Pulse: measure how much ikigai you feel now, across the seven needs psychiatrist Mieko Kamiya linked to it.
• Remember: recall the moments when life felt most worth living.
• Notice: collect your small daily joys and see what fills and drains your week.
• Gather: name your sources of ikigai across eight areas of life, and test which are truly yours.
• Weave: find the threads that run through it all and write your ikigai in your own words.
• Live: start small with one-week experiments and a gentle daily rhythm.

Your finished atlas maps every source of ikigai, shows which needs are fed and which are hungry, and points out patterns in your own words. Every result is unique, because it's built only from what you write.

Built on research, not the internet meme. The framework draws on Mieko Kamiya, Gordon Mathews, Ken Mogi and Akihiro Hasegawa. The famous four-circle diagram is included as an optional work lens, and the app explains where it really came from.

Private by design. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your answers stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Optional pattern reading runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

Also included: reminders for your morning and evening questions, a pulse history, Face ID lock, and export as PDF or text.
