# Ikigai Atlas privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

Ikigai Atlas helps you reflect on what makes your life worth living. What you write is personal, so the app is built to keep it that way.

## What we collect

Nothing. Ikigai Atlas has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your answers to us or to anyone else.

## Where your answers are stored

- **On your iPhone.** Your atlas is saved in the app's private storage on your device.
- **In your own iCloud, if you use iCloud.** When you are signed in to iCloud, your atlas syncs through your private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account. We cannot see it. You can turn off iCloud for the app in the Settings app at any time.

## Pattern reading

The optional "Look for patterns" feature uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Your answers are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple Intelligence works.

## Reminders

Morning, evening and review reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

## Exports and backups

If you export your atlas as a PDF, text or backup file, the file goes wherever you choose to send or save it. We never receive a copy.

## Deleting your data

In the app, open **Settings → Start a fresh atlas** to erase your answers from your iPhone and from your iCloud database. Deleting the app also removes the data stored on your device.

## Children

Ikigai Atlas is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
